# Tasting Room and Events: Staff Reference

A sample document written to demonstrate the Document Q&A tool. It is not
official Reid's Distillery policy. Replace it with your real SOPs, AGCO
paperwork, recipe specs and venue policies.

## Experiences

### Signature Distillery Tour and Tasting
Runs as a group experience and lasts approximately 90 minutes. Guests are walked
through the botanical selection, the still, and the cut, finishing with a
cocktail in the lounge. Sold online and as a $51 gift voucher.

### Reid's Cocktail Class
Open to all experience levels, including complete beginners. Guests build three
cocktails and drink them in the lounge afterward. Instructors cover shaking
versus stirring, dilution, and garnish technique. Sold online and as a $51 gift
voucher.

### Reid's Sunday High Tea
A seated experience pairing gin cocktails with a tea service. Sold as a $51 gift
voucher.

### Virtual Tasting
A free 30-minute online session, useful for remote corporate groups or for
customers deciding between bottles before buying.

## Serving Non-Drinking Guests

Every cocktail on the class menu has a zero-alcohol version built on Seedlip in
place of the gin. Staff should offer this proactively rather than waiting to be
asked. The same mixers, syrups and garnishes are used, so the drink looks and is
served identically.

Stock check before a class: confirm at least one Seedlip expression is on hand.
Grove 42 and Spice 94 are currently out of stock, so Garden 108 is the working
default.

## Private Events and Weddings

Enquiries should be answered within one business day, even if the answer is only
to acknowledge and ask for the date and headcount. The three details needed
before quoting are date, guest count, and whether the client wants a seated or
standing format.

Do not quote a minimum spend by email without checking the current figure with
the events lead.

## Gift Vouchers

Vouchers are purchased through the online store. Recipients select their own
date subject to availability. Confirm the current expiry terms before telling a
customer a voucher does not expire.

## Botanical Notes for Tour Talking Points

Signature Gin leads on juniper with coriander seed, angelica root and orris root
as the supporting structure. Orris root is used in small quantities as a fixative
that holds the aromatics together. Citrus Gin adds fresh lemon and orange peel.
Spiced Gin brings cardamom and cassia bark forward. Navy Strength is bottled at
higher proof, which carries the botanicals further in a cocktail.

## Cocktail Class Consumables

Each class consumes citrus garnishes, syrups and juice mixes. Dehydrated citrus
slices are used for garnish on the finished drinks. If dehydrated stock is out,
fresh-cut citrus is the fallback and should be prepped before guests arrive.

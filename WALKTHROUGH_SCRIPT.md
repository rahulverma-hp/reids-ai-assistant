# Notes for the demo video

Aim for 90 seconds to two minutes. Record the screen with voiceover, no editing
needed. Loom or the Xbox Game Bar (Win+G) both work.

Talk about the distillery's problems, not the code. The audience is a small
business owner, not an engineer.

## Opening

Say who you are, what you're applying for, and that you built a working prototype
rather than a description of one. Mention that everything on screen uses their real
product lineup and prices.

## Event enquiries

Load the corporate booking sample. Point out that it asks about 14 people, a
Thursday, and mentions guests who don't drink.

Click draft. While it generates, say that it only answers from an offerings sheet
they can edit, so it can't invent a price.

When the draft lands, cover two things:

- It recommended the cocktail class and said why.
- It raised the non-alcoholic options without being asked.

Then scroll to the flagged section and say the important part: it doesn't know
their minimum spend or room capacity, so it flagged those instead of guessing. A
tool that quotes a price nobody approved is worse than one that leaves a blank.

## Inventory

Show the split between production and retail and explain why it exists. Short on
angelica root and a bottling run stalls. Short on tonic and you lose sales today.
Those are different problems.

Scroll to the stockout cost. Give the total, then say plainly that it's an estimate
with one assumed input. Move the slider so they see the figure respond, and say
that when they plug in their real sales rate it becomes a measurement.

Worth naming out loud: thirteen items listed as sold out at once, including all
four citrus garnishes and two of three Seedlips.

## Document Q&A

Click the orris root question. When the answer appears, point at the citation and
say that every answer names the document behind it, so nothing has to be taken on
trust.

Then ask something the documents don't cover and let it refuse. That refusal is the
point of showing this tab.

## Closing

Say the code and tests are on GitHub, that you'd want to start by replacing the
sample data with their real export, and that you'd like to talk about it.
